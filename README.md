# Sales-Analytics-
This is a Retail / Sales Analytics Dashboard for a company named SN Corp. The dashboard tracks sales performance across different customers, products, regions, countries, and shipping modes. It helps management monitor revenue, costs, profits, customer performance, and sales trends over time.

![sales](https://github.com/shiva2334/Sales-analysis/assets/65301053/ce73ea9d-e82d-4602-9e0a-fba408c236a5)

Key Metrics
Total Sales: 1.3M
Total Cost: 640.6K
Total Profit: 627K

This indicates that the company is operating profitably with nearly 48-50% profit margin.

4 Key Business Insights
1. The business is highly profitable

Total Sales are 1.3M while Total Cost is only 640.6K, resulting in approximately 627K profit. This shows strong profitability and good cost management.

2. Bookcases, Chairs, and Appliances are the top-selling product categories

From the KPIs by Sub Categories chart, Bookcases generate the highest sales, followed by Chairs and Appliances. These products contribute the most revenue and should be prioritized for inventory and marketing efforts.

3. Central region contributes the highest quantity sold

The Quantity Sold by Region chart shows that the Central region accounts for about 55% of total quantity sold, significantly higher than North and South regions. This indicates the Central region is the company's strongest market.

4. Economy shipping mode is used the most

The Total Orders by Quarter & Shipping Mode trend shows Economy shipping consistently has the highest order volume compared to Economy Plus, Priority, and Immediate shipping. Customers appear to prefer lower-cost shipping options.
